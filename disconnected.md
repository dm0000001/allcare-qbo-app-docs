# QuickBooks Connection Disconnected

The QuickBooks Online connection for Allcare Internal QuickBooks Automation has been disconnected.

When disconnected, the application can no longer access or create records in the connected QuickBooks Online company account.

## What This Means

The internal workflow may be unable to:
- Create invoices in QuickBooks Online
- Access required QuickBooks company data
- Complete invoice automation steps that depend on QuickBooks access
- Continue normal billing automation until reconnected

## Reconnecting

To reconnect the application, an authorized company user must return to the connection page and authorize access to the correct QuickBooks Online company account.
