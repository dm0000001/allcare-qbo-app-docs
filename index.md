# Allcare Internal QuickBooks Automation

Allcare Internal QuickBooks Automation is a private internal workflow used by authorized personnel of Allcare Nursing Services, Inc. to support billing, invoice review, invoice creation, and accounting workflow automation with QuickBooks Online.

This application helps support internal processes such as:

- Reviewing Fieldglass invoice activity
- Identifying invoices that may need to be created in QuickBooks Online
- Creating QuickBooks invoices from approved internal workflow reports
- Maintaining workflow logs for review and troubleshooting
- Supporting billing reconciliation and accounting accuracy

This application is not available for public use. Access is limited to authorized company personnel only.
