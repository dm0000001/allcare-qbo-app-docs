# Connect to QuickBooks

This page is used by authorized Allcare Nursing Services, Inc. personnel to connect or reconnect Allcare Internal QuickBooks Automation to QuickBooks Online.

The connection allows the internal workflow to support invoice creation, billing review, accounting automation, and reconciliation processes.

## Before Connecting

Please confirm that:
- You are authorized to connect company applications to QuickBooks Online
- You are signing in to the correct QuickBooks Online company account
- You understand that the application may create or process invoice-related accounting records
- You have reviewed the application’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Use
