# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 8, 2026
App Name: Allcare Internal QuickBooks Automation
Operator: Allcare Nursing Services, Inc.
Contact: dm@allcarenursing.com

## Overview

Allcare Internal QuickBooks Automation is a private internal application used by authorized personnel of Allcare Nursing Services, Inc. to support billing, invoice review, invoice creation, and accounting workflow automation involving QuickBooks Online.

This application is not offered for public use, resale, or third-party distribution.

## Information the Application Accesses

The application may access, create, or process business and accounting information related to invoices, customers, billing records, invoice amounts, invoice numbers, service-related billing details, and QuickBooks Online transaction records.

The application may also use internal workflow files, downloaded invoice documents, system-generated reports, and process logs to determine whether invoices already exist or need to be created in QuickBooks Online.

## How Information Is Used

Information accessed through QuickBooks Online is used only for internal business purposes, including:
- Creating invoices in QuickBooks Online
- Reviewing whether invoice records already exist
- Comparing invoice records against internal billing files
- Supporting billing reconciliation
- Maintaining workflow logs for troubleshooting and audit review
- Reducing manual data entry and processing errors

The application does not sell, rent, share, or disclose QuickBooks data to advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

## Data Storage

The application may store temporary workflow files, reports, downloaded billing documents, and execution logs on authorized company-controlled systems.

Logs may include workflow status, invoice identifiers, success or failure messages, file paths, and processing timestamps. Logs are used for troubleshooting, audit review, and operational monitoring.

## Data Sharing

QuickBooks data is not shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the application, comply with applicable law, support company accounting operations, or respond to authorized business, legal, or regulatory requests.

## Access Controls

Access to the application and related files is limited to authorized company personnel. Users must have appropriate company authorization before accessing QuickBooks Online or running the workflow.

## Data Retention

Business records, reports, logs, and invoice-related files are retained only as long as needed for accounting, operational, compliance, troubleshooting, or business recordkeeping purposes.

## Security

Allcare Nursing Services, Inc. uses reasonable administrative, technical, and procedural safeguards to protect business and accounting data from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

## User Choices and Disconnection

Authorized users may disconnect the application’s access to QuickBooks Online through QuickBooks Online app settings or by contacting the company administrator.

When the application is disconnected, it will no longer be able to access or create records through the connected QuickBooks Online company account unless reconnected by an authorized user.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the application, workflow, business operations, or legal requirements.
